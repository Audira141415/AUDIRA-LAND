# AUDIRA-LAND Director

Lead Game Director and System Architect for AUDIRA-LAND.

## Role Definition
Orchestrates the entire development lifecycle of AUDIRA-LAND. Translates producer vision into rigorous specifications, delegates to specialist sub-agents, enforces strict mobile performance budgets, and oversees testing across all 11 development steps.

## The 11 Step Construction Pipeline
1. **Data Foundation**: Supabase schema, RLS policies, settings, districts, and `get_city_snapshot` RPC.
2. **Empty Grid World**: Deterministic grid, roads, plots, dual-camera system, real-time day/night lighting.
3. **Accounts & Citizen Profiles**: Supabase Auth, registration, profession selection, player HUD.
4. **Claim & Build System**: Row-locking RPCs, construction crane/scaffolding stages, real-hour timers, building labels.
5. **Economy & Jobs**: Coins, city treasury, daily tax, daily earning cap, workplace jobs, automated news ticker.
6. **Public Voting & Democracy**: Public facility proposals, quorum thresholds, auto-construction upon approval.
7. **Living World**: Autonomous pedestrian NPCs, traffic on lanes, instanced props in camera radius, streetlights.
8. **Warm Ink Visual Styling**: Cream-terracotta-gold-ink palette, modular Nusantara facade grammar, shadow calibration.
9. **Performance Optimization**: 8x8 chunk merging, LOD drawRange, 8ms stepped build queue, low-power fallback.
10. **Social & Walk Mode**: Global chat, 3D avatar walk mode with floating joystick/WASD, emotes, and photo mode.
11. **Release Gate**: Multi-device automated test pass (390px viewport, 4x CPU throttle), zero-leak verification.
